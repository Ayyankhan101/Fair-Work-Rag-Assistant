Introduction
              We publish decisions and orders as soon as the Commission issues them. This includes outside of standard business hours. Use keywords and filters to refine your search.

          

    
  
  
  
    
  
    Content
          
                
                
        
            **Decisions** issued since 1 January 2003 are available in our document search. **Orders and delegates decisions** issued since 1 January 2013 (and some older orders) are also available. 

You can access earlier decisions and orders from [AustLII's databases](https://www.austlii.edu.au/databases.html).

We also publish:

- documents about [major cases](/hearings-decisions/major-cases)
- [significant decisions](/hearings-decisions/decisions-and-orders/significant-decisions-and-summaries), and
- [national wage and safety net review decisions and summaries](/hearings-decisions/decisions-and-orders/national-wage-and-safety-net-review-decisions).
